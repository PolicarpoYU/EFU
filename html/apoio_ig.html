How to support the EFU project

EFU is an independent project created to offer a simple, fast and easy-to-use emoji keyboard.

The project was developed by Policarpo Yoshin Ulianov with technical support from ChatGPT-5.

At the moment, the project does not have fixed sponsorship. The goal is to keep the site clean, light and free from invasive advertising.

Any support received helps directly with hosting, maintenance, testing, improvements and promotion of EFU.

Ways to support

• Financial donation, to help cover system costs.

• Sharing the project with other people.

• Business sponsorship, with discreet visibility on the site.
